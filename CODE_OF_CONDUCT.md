# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We judge contributions on their content. Expected: welcoming and inclusive language,
respect for differing viewpoints, constructive criticism, empathy, credit where due.
Unacceptable: harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or
political attacks, publishing others' private information, or any conduct a reasonable
professional would consider inappropriate in a workplace.

## Scope

Project spaces (repo, issues, PRs, chats) and public representation of the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainers (contact in SECURITY.md). Acknowledgment within
72h. Consequences: private warning, temporary ban, permanent ban, proportionate to
severity and recurrence. Appeals go to maintainers not involved in the decision.
